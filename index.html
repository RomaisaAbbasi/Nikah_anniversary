My Dearest Taimoor,

Happy Anniversary, my love. ❤️

It still feels a little funny to me how life works sometimes. I never imagined that you would become such a big part of my life, let alone the person I would end up wanting beside me for the rest of it.

And honestly, one of the things I love most about us is that I got to discover you slowly. I knew you, but I didn't really know you. And then, after we became husband and wife, I got to see the little things — how calm you are, how patient you are, how naturally loving you are, and how much softness there is in you. Those are the things that made me fall for you even more.

I love how easy it is to be with you. We can talk about something completely random, have serious conversations about our future, laugh at stupid things, or I can simply annoy you with my kalesh 😂. Somehow, all of it feels like us.

I love your heart, your nature, your patience, your kindness, and the way you love without making a big show of it. I love your eyes, your smile, your hugs, and that feeling I get when you're close to me. There is something about being in your arms that makes everything feel a little more right. And yes, I miss your hugs and kisses more than I probably tell you. ❤️

Maybe that's the hardest part of loving you from a distance — there are so many ordinary little moments I wish I could have with you. Sitting together, talking about nothing, annoying each other, falling asleep next to each other, stealing little hugs and kisses whenever we can. I miss you, but I also miss the simple life that feels so much sweeter when you're in it.

With you, I have this strange feeling of completeness. Not because you complete something that is missing in me, but because when we are together, everything just feels right. Like this is where I'm supposed to be.

And when I think about our future, I don't imagine anything perfect or extraordinary. I imagine us — our home, our little routines, our silly arguments, our laughter, our family, and growing old together through all the normal days of life.

Thank you for being the person you are. For loving me, understanding me, putting up with me, and for giving me a love that feels so warm and real.

I don't know what life has planned for us, but I know one thing — I want to experience all of it with you.

I love you, Taimoor. More than I sometimes know how to put into words.

Happy Anniversary, my Cutu. ❤️

Forever yours,
Hoorain
